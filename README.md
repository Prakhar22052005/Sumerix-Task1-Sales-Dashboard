# 📊 Sales & Target Performance Dashboard

## 🎯 Executive Summary
This repository contains an end-to-end **Sales & Target Performance Dashboard** developed in Microsoft Excel as part of the **Sumerix Global Data Analytics & BI Internship (Task-1)**. 

The primary objective of this project is to clean, transform, and visualize multi-variable retail data to assist executive leadership in tracking sales targets, regional growth, manager efficiency, and product sales dynamics.

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales Revenue:** 71,178,664
- **Total Target Revenue:** 41,693,329
- **Total Orders Processed:** 500
- **Overall Target Achievement:** 170.72%
- **Total Units Sold:** 2,716 Units

---

## 📈 Visual Highlights & Analysis Breakdown
1. **Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart):** Compares total monthly sales against target benchmarks across the entire calendar year.
2. **Sales by Region (Column Chart):** Highlights regional revenue distributions (*East, North, South, West*).
3. **Sales by Manager (Horizontal Bar Chart):** Evaluates individual sales performance across all 8 team managers (`Manager_1` through `Manager_8`).
4. **Category Contribution (Donut Chart):** Visualizes revenue breakdown across product lines:
   - **Electronics:** 28%
   - **Accessories:** 25%
   - **Clothing:** 24%
   - **Furniture:** 22%
5. **Top 10 Products by Sales (Bar Chart):** Analyzes highest grossing items including *Headphones, Laptops, Shirts, Phones, Chairs, Shoes, Tables, and Watches*.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Elements & Filtering
- **Region Slicer:** Filters entire dashboard by geographic zones (*East, North, South, West*).
- **Product Category Slicer:** Cross-examines specific product categories (*Clothing, Electronics, Furniture*).
- **Month Timeline Slicer:** Offers multi-month granularity from *Jan* through *Dec*.
